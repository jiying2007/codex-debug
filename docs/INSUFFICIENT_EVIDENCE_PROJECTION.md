# Insufficient-evidence projection security contract

Promotion Corpus v2 may attach at most one digest-bound insufficient-evidence evaluation variant to a unique reviewed historical transition. The variant never creates another reviewed transition for the 12-case floor and never creates another Promotion Provenance transition.

The only supported projection is `summary-only` version 1. It is generated by the controller; corpus authors cannot supply custom model-visible text or hints. The variant must expect `insufficient`, use `patchPolicy=forbidden`, and expose no root-cause terms.

Model-visible projected evidence contains only failure kind, run/failure/timeout counts and the reproduction-command digest. It must not contain raw stdout/stderr, source anchors, Git history, case identity, variant identity, fixed SHA, ground-truth summary or ground-truth files. `includeGitHistory=false` is mandatory and a pre-model leak check fails closed.

The underlying transition is still qualified with full controller evidence: exact reviewed bad commit must reproduce and its direct-child reviewed fix must pass the same command. Ground truth remains evaluator-side only.

The current corpus contains eight unique reviewed transitions and three authentic insufficient variants attached to three of those transitions. That produces eleven model-evaluation views but only eight reviewed cases. The insufficient structural floor is therefore `3/3`, while the unique reviewed-case floor remains `8/12`; overall promotion readiness remains false.

These three variants come from real reviewed fixes in Codex Debug, Safe Core and Codex Diagnose. Additional Change and Diagnose streaming transitions expand reviewed coverage without duplicating variants or provenance. Their presence proves the corpus/evidence contract and negative-example structure only. It does **not** prove live-model insufficient-evidence accuracy until a credential-backed live model-evaluation artifact is recorded.
